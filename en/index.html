FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate with our CRM if it has no API?

Yes. A Chrome extension works right inside the CRM interface, filling in and transferring data even if the system has no public API.

Can a bot or Mini App be connected to our website and CRM?

Yes. Requests from the bot, Mini App and website land in your CRM, and postbacks go to your tracker — it all works as one system, with no manual data transfer.

Which trackers do you work with?

Keitaro and Binom: we set up correct postback delivery and connect the tracker to your CRM, bot or website. Using a different tracker? Message us and we’ll talk it through.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope — we give you a timeline in our first conversation, along with the price. You see a working prototype before the final polish, so you can track the result as we go.

How is the price calculated?

Yes, if you want it. The cost depends on the project size and workload — if you need support, we’ll estimate and agree on it before we start.
